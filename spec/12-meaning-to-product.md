# Meaning to product

## Foundation

Firdawsi is an experience-first design language. Jannah-inspired qualities inform
its welcome, nearness, ease, and peaceful completion. Intention, dignity,
gentleness, truthfulness, and trust inform its ethical practice. Architecture and
geometry are optional expressions, not required metaphors or substitutes for usable behaviour.

The religious source, its meaning and original context, and the proposed design
interpretation MUST remain distinguishable. Religious connections are not claims
that revelation prescribes software controls, timings, colours, or layouts.
Sources have been checked; no scholarly endorsement of the design interpretations
is claimed. Sacred text belongs to explanatory documentation, never ornamental
texture, button labels, loading copy, or completion feedback.

## Prior work and what Firdawsi takes from it

- [Nasruddin and Hussin, *Muslim Centred user interface design prototype* (2014)](https://ir.uitm.edu.my/id/eprint/70450/) describe a culture-centred framework with designer and user filters. Their prototype drew on Islamic cultural material, including Qur’an and hadith, and was evaluated with students. Their [later, full MCUID paper (2022)](https://www.istes.org/seeder/books/files/70a06283965f27a3f824dac257f774fc.pdf) reports a 26-person icon-recognition exercise; 17 associated its tested “Bismillah” icon with Start, while 9 selected other actions. Firdawsi takes the research question of whether a designer's assumptions match users' mental models, not the study's religious phrases as generic interface controls.
- [Riasat Islam, *Design Thinking from an Islamic Perspective* (2024)](https://www.riasatislam.com/blog/design-thinking/) connects empathy, purposeful ideation, iteration, consultation, and ethical responsibility to Islamic thought. Firdawsi uses these as prompts for research and critique: speak with affected people, make prototypes, observe use, and revise. The article's religious references are not treated as independently verified source text for Firdawsi principles.
- [Nader Ardalan and Laleh Bakhtiar, *The Sense of Unity* (1973)](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Sense_of_Unity.html?id=g7A3jwEACAAJ) examines relationships among units and larger wholes in Persian Islamic architecture. A [CEPT University library review](https://cept.ac.in/UserFiles/File/03%20CEPT%20University%202016/02%20-%20News/10%20-%20Oct/CEPT-bookrev-PRINT-1.pdf) outlines its concepts, forms, and city-scale sections. Firdawsi takes the compositional question of coherence across scales into its visual review; no particular diagram or rule from the unread full book is reproduced.

Together these precedents inform three distinct practices: research cultural context and test mental models; consult and iterate with people affected by the design; review whether component, screen, and journey feel coherent. Each Firdawsi source-to-behaviour inference still needs its own citation, context, product example, and evaluation. New scriptural principles require owner review before inclusion.

The fuller extraction, evidence limits, and component-level applications are in [From prior work to design practice](18-precedents-to-design-system.md).

## Twelve principles

| Principle | Religious source | Proposed product contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intention | [Bukhari 1](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1) | State the user need before selecting the visual treatment. |
| Tranquillity (طمأنينة) | [Ar-Ra'd 13:28](https://quran.com/ar-rad/28) | Make commitment, waiting, and completion understandable. The verse concerns spiritual comfort through remembrance of Allah; this product contract is a limited design analogy. |
| Welcome (الترحيب) | [Az-Zumar 39:73](https://quran.com/az-zumar/73) | Explain what is needed to begin and what happens next; reduce needless barriers. The verse describes the hereafter; this is a bounded design question about welcome and clear entry. |
| Rest (راحة) | [Al-Hijr 15:48](https://quran.com/al-hijr/48) | Let people pause long tasks and resume without losing their place. The verse describes the hereafter; this is a limited design question about avoidable fatigue and session pacing. |
| Ask before access (الاستئذان قبل الوصول) | [An-Nur 24:27](https://quran.com/an-nur/27) | Explain why a device capability or private data is needed, ask first, and respect refusal. The verse concerns permission before entering others' homes; applying it to software access is a bounded design inference. |
| Fair dealing (الإنصاف) | [Al-Mutaffifin 83:1–3](https://quran.com/al-mutaffifin/1-3) | Show the full price, renewal terms, included benefits, and material limits before commitment. The passage condemns dishonest measuring in transactions; applying it to product disclosure is a design inference. |
| Consultation (الشورى) | [Ash-Shuraa 42:38](https://quran.com/ash-shuraa/38) | Involve affected people in research and review, then revise confusing experiences. The verse praises mutual consultation; it does not prescribe a UX research method. |
| Nearness | [Al-Insan 76:14](https://quran.com/al-insan/14) | Relevant action and recovery stay beside their context. |
| Ease | [Bukhari 69](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:69) | Preserve work, avoid repeated entry, and offer direct recovery. |
| Beauty | [Muslim 91a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:91a) | Coherent proportion and hierarchy precede ornament; reject contempt and exclusion. |
| Dignity | [Al-Isra 17:70](https://quran.com/al-isra/70) | Support touch, keyboard, assistive technology, and authored language experiences. |
| Restraint | [Al-Waqi‘ah 56:25–26](https://quran.com/al-waqiah/25-26) | Use purposeful motion and feedback, then return to stillness. |

Jannah passages are experiential inspiration, not depictions of the unseen.
Az-Zumar 39:73 describes arrival in Jannah and its keepers' greeting; it is not a prescription for interface gates or access control.
Al-Hijr 15:48 describes the hereafter; our application concerns reducing avoidable effort in long tasks, not claiming an interface removes fatigue.
An-Nur 24:27 concerns entering others' homes; it does not prescribe software permission flows.
Ar-Ra'd 13:28 concerns spiritual comfort through remembrance of Allah; it is not a claim that an interface creates that comfort.
Bukhari 69 concerns religious guidance; usability is an ethical extension.
Muslim 91a discusses fine clothing and pride, not a mandated visual style.
Restraint is an analogy about attention, not a ruling against decoration.

## Interaction and foundation connections

- Rest: 56:25–26; stable and readable defaults.
- Approach: 76:14; discoverable contextual actions.
- Response: [Muslim 2594a](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2594a); gentle feedback.
- Continuity: Bukhari 69; preserve context and orientation.
- Settling: Muslim 2594a; movement reaches a stable result.
- Completion: [Al-Hijr 15:46](https://quran.com/al-hijr/46); a peaceful, secure arrival is a limited design analogy.
- Truthful state reporting: [Al-Ahzab 33:70](https://quran.com/al-ahzab/70); describe what happened accurately.
- Prepared surfaces: [Al-Ghashiyah 88:13–16](https://legacy.quran.com/88/10-16);
  arranged furnishings inspire readiness without literal visual imitation.
- Typography and localisation: [Ar-Rum 30:22](https://quran.com/ar-rum/22);
  respect for linguistic diversity motivates independent optical tuning.
- Focus and contrast: 17:70; dignity motivates usable access.
- Data and recovery: [An-Nisa 4:58](https://quran.com/an-nisa/58);
  fulfilling trusts motivates preservation of drafts and truthful recovery.
- Evaluation: [Al-Isra 17:36](https://quran.com/al-isra/36);
  knowledge before claims motivates source checks and practical testing.

## Implemented reference scope

The showcase philosophy section has English/Arabic content, source links,
Arabic excerpts, context disclosures, a source register, and the six-stage model.
The experience lab demonstrates three complete flows:

1. Capture a task: trim input, retain selected day, persist locally, confirm only
   after storage succeeds. Connection: ease (Bukhari 69) and trust (4:58).
2. Move within a week: use a native select accessible by keyboard; persist the
   day and expose undo. Connection: dignity (17:70) and gentleness (Muslim 2594a).
3. Recover from failure: preserve old tasks and the capture draft, expose retry
   and cancellation, and never claim success on a storage error. Connection:
   truthfulness (33:70) and trust (4:58).

Invalid saved data is not overwritten. While a failed mutation is pending, other
mutations are disabled until retry or cancellation, avoiding accidental loss.
Three visual directions share identical data and functionality. Decorative
geometry is optional and absent by default. Sacred excerpts remain outside the
working task interface. These are reference interactions, not a complete planner
or an integration into Misho.

## Validation

Run the repository unit tests, production build, type checks, and Playwright suite.
The philosophy suite covers sources, independent Arabic direction, storage failures,
retry without duplication, keyboard movement, persistence, undo, corrupt data,
visual treatment switching, and narrow RTL high-contrast layouts.

Automated checks do not constitute full accessibility conformance or scholarly
review. Formative user studies, expert Arabic review, expert accessibility review,
and adoption in a real product remain follow-on work. The prototype alone does
not establish that a design direction is recognisable or successful.
