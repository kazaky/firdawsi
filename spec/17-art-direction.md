# Week 8 — Refine the design language

The current priority is artistic quality: philosophy, geometry, patterns, typography and their composition. Shareable component distribution remains deferred.

## Direction

Use warm ivory reading surfaces, mineral greens, restrained brass accents and generous intervals. Large Latin display typography uses Cormorant Garamond; Arabic display uses El Messiri. Noto Sans and Noto Sans Arabic keep long-form reading steady. These are contemporary design choices, not claims about sacred geometry or historical authenticity.

The opening pairs a clear statement with a dedicated sixteen-fold medallion. Sacred text remains in its explanatory context and never becomes ornament. A separate twelve-fold colour study reveals its construction on request. Malachite, Lapis and Clay change the artwork and gallery together, without changing semantic interface colours.

## Composition rules

- Keep text and artwork in separate spaces, with no patterns behind body copy.
- Give patterns a clear centre, restrained stroke weights and generous margins.
- Present construction lines as an optional learning layer.
- Use expressive display sizes and comfortable body line heights, preserving Arabic shaping.
- Stack compositions on narrow screens; keep palette and construction controls keyboard accessible.
- Preserve theme, direction, reduced-motion and task-flow behaviour.

## Verification

Production build and existing automated accessibility, reflow and interaction checks remain release gates. The new study checks that colour selection updates the artwork and that construction can be revealed independently. A human visual review remains necessary for aesthetic judgement.
