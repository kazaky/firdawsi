# Week 8 — Coherence carries identity

The owner rejected the initial muted green / Latin serif refinement. This revision restores the original bilingual display family and makes the geometric language visible throughout the interface. Artistic quality remains the priority; shareable component distribution is deferred.

## One voice, two scripts

El Messiri is the display family for both Arabic and Latin. Match weight and optical presence; retain natural Arabic shaping and generous Arabic leading. Noto Sans and Noto Sans Arabic handle sustained reading. Cormorant Garamond has been removed.

## Colour with energy

Cobalt is the primary action and identity colour. Violet adds depth, coral brings warmth to artwork, and porcelain neutrals hold reading content. Green remains a semantic success colour, not the identity. Colour choices are contemporary interpretations, not religious requirements or historical reconstructions.

Canonical light, dark and high-contrast tokens drive the interface. Optional regional examples preserve their named context. Historical reference plates are distinguished from new artwork.

## Exact repeat, expressive treatment

The new eight-fold field uses a star and its complementary cross on the same 100-unit grid. With half-cell a = 50, b = a / √2 and c = a − b, the first quadrant of the star is (a,0), (b,c), (b,b), (c,b), repeated through quarter-turns. The cross follows those same boundaries from neighbouring stars. Controlled facet tones and a small coral centre create depth without arbitrary overlapping motifs.

Construction guides are optional. No sacred text enters the artwork.

## Identity in use

- A softened facet recurs in controls, selection groups, cards and navigation.
- The eight-fold centre becomes a selection marker; state is also conveyed through text and native semantics.
- The colour study changes the hero and gallery artwork together.
- The intention study demonstrates selection, action, confirmation and reset with keyboard-operable controls.
- Existing task capture, edit, completion, undo and recovery remain functional.
- Focus rings remain outside usable hit areas. Reduced motion removes transitions; narrow layouts stack rather than clip.

## Validation

Production build, unit tests and browser accessibility / reflow checks are release gates. Automated checks do not substitute for visual approval. User-supplied screenshots informed the typography correction; live visual inspection remains unavailable in this session.
